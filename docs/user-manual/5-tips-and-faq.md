# 5. Tips and common questions

[Back to contents](README.md)

## Signing in

**I can't sign in.**
Check that you chose the right tab (**Job Seeker**, **Employer**, or **Admin**) before typing your email and password. The app has no "forgot password" option; if you still cannot sign in, contact PESO.

**The app says "Network error" or "Backend could not be reached".**
The phone cannot reach the PESO-Link server (the computer online that keeps everyone's accounts and data; "backend" means the same here). Check your mobile data or Wi-Fi, then try again. If it still fails, tell the PESO-Link team.

**I was signed out by myself.**
This happens when your sign-in expires or when PESO deactivates the account. Sign in again, or contact PESO if the account was deactivated.

## NSRP profile and form scan

**What do the photo tips mean?**
Take one page per photo, with the form flat, in good and even light, and the whole page in view. Paper forms read best; photos of a screen are harder.

**The scan filled in a wrong answer.**
The scan only helps with typing and can misread handwriting. Fix the field before tapping **CONFIRM AND SAVE**. Fields marked **Check:** need extra attention, but a field without a note can still be wrong.

**Do I have to use the scan?**
No. You can type the whole profile yourself.

**The scan is taking long.**
Reading a photo can take up to about a minute, longer if several people scan at the same time. Wait for "OCR Complete". If it fails, try a clearer photo or type the answers.

**The SAVE AND SUBMIT TO PESO button is grey.**
All 19 required items must be filled in and the certification box ticked. The **Required NSRP items** card on **Profile** lists what is still missing.

**I can't find a button.**
Many screens are longer than the phone screen. Scroll down; buttons like **SAVE PROFILE** and **CONFIRM AND SAVE** are at the bottom.

**Why are my answers in capital letters?**
NSRP Form 1 asks for block letters, so the app saves the answers in capitals. Emails, phone numbers, and dates stay as typed.

**Do the Section VIII checkboxes count in the skill comparison?**
No. Only the skills you pick from the **Skills** list on your profile are compared with each job's required skills.

## Applying

**Why can't I apply?**
Your NSRP profile must be **PESO-Verified** first. If you changed a verified profile, wait until PESO verifies it again. The job page shows your current status.

**What is the difference between PESO-Verified and PESO-Referred?**
PESO verifies the **person** (the NSRP profile) once. After that, every **application** the person sends is PESO-referred.

**My application says Closed. Was I rejected?**
No. Closed means the job post was closed (or an account was deactivated) before a decision was made. You may apply to other jobs.

**Does the skill comparison decide who gets hired?**
No. It only lists matched and missing skills. The employer decides the application status.

## Alerts

**Will I get a text message or email?**
No. All alerts are inside the app, in the **Alerts** tab. A red number on the tab shows unread alerts.
